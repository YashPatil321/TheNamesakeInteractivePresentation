# The Namesake: Video Plan (Short Version)

**7 scenes spread across the whole story · 25–30 seconds each · filmed in one afternoon at one house**
**Total time: about 1½ hours of filming and 2 hours of editing**

The website already shows the year, title and story for every scene, so the videos don't need title cards, narration or fancy transitions. Just act the moment.

## Scene 1: Two Names at School (1973) · ~30 sec
1. **ASHOKE** (kneeling to Gogol's height): "At school, you'll be Nikhil."
   **GOGOL** (arms crossed): "No. I'm Gogol."
2. **PRINCIPAL** (at a desk, shrugs): "Gogol it is." Writes it down.
3. Close-up: the **GOGOL** name tag.

## Scene 2: "I'm Nikhil" (1986) · ~25 sec
**Setup:** dim room, colored light or party music. Use a cool blue filter.
1. **KIM:** "Hey, what's your name?"
2. **GOGOL** hesitates, then says: "…Nikhil." Kim smiles.
3. Close-up: a hand crosses out **GOGOL** on the name tag and writes **NIKHIL**.

## Scene 3: Dinner with the Ratliffs (1990s) · ~30 sec
**Setup:** a dinner table with plates. Nikhil sits with Maxine and a guest.
1. Everyone is laughing. Nikhil looks relaxed and happy.
2. **GUEST:** "You must never get sick when you go to India!"
3. **NIKHIL** (smile fading): "I'm from Massachusetts." Awkward silence. Hold on his face.

## Scene 4: The Phone Call (1990s) · ~25 sec · *no dialogue*
**Setup:** any room, then a bathroom mirror. Use a grey, cold filter.
1. His phone rings. He answers, listens, and slowly sits down.
2. He folds his father's shirts into a box.
3. At the mirror he raises a razor to his hair. **Cut before it touches.**

## Scene 5: Both of My Names (late 1990s) · ~30 sec · *the most important one*
**Setup:** two chairs at a small table, two glasses, a candle or phone light. Evening.
1. Nikhil and Moushumi sit down across from each other, a little awkward.
2. **MOUSHUMI:** "My mother made me come tonight." **NIKHIL:** "Mine too." They both laugh.
3. **MOUSHUMI:** "I remember you as Gogol." **NIKHIL** (pauses): "Everyone calls me Nikhil now." **MOUSHUMI** (smiling): "I'll remember both."
4. Close-up: they clink glasses. Hold 2 seconds.

## Scene 6: Coming Apart (~2000) · ~30 sec
**Setup:** a table at night with one lamp on. Use a grey, cold filter.
1. Moushumi laughs quietly on the phone, sees Nikhil, and hangs up fast.
2. **NIKHIL:** "Who was that?" **MOUSHUMI** (long pause, not looking at him): "Dimitri."
3. Nikhil sets his wedding ring on the table and walks out.
4. Close-up: the ring on the table. Hold 3 seconds, fade out.

## Scene 7: The Last Party (2000) · ~30 sec
**Setup:** Christmas Eve. Lights on, music, a few people talking in the background.
1. **ASHIMA** (raising a glass): "After tonight this house belongs to someone else. I'll be in Calcutta half the year." Sonia hugs her.
2. **Gogol** stands apart, picks up a framed photo of his father, and looks at it.
3. He sets it down and quietly climbs the stairs. **Cut as he reaches the top.** (The website takes over from there with the book.)

*All dialogue is our own, written by the group.*

## Filming order (about 1½ hours)
1. **House: living room, stairs + mirror (25 min):** Scenes 7 and 4
2. **Desk + dinner table (45 min):** Scenes 1, 3, 5 and 6
3. **Dim room (15 min):** Scene 2

## Editing (CapCut, ~15–20 min per video)
- Trim the start and end, pick the best take, and add blue to Scene 2 and grey to Scenes 4 and 6.
- Export as **MP4, 720p**.

## Tips
- Do **2 takes** of each line, not more.
- Hold the phone **sideways** and steady. Lean it on something.
- Stand **close** to whoever is talking so the audio is clear.
