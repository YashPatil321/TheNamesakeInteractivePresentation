# The Namesake: Video Planning Doc

8 short scenes for the website's **Scene** tabs. Each is **45–90 seconds** and filmed on a phone in **landscape**.
Save each final video in `videos/` with the filename listed so it appears on the site automatically.

> **Short on time?** Film only the ⭐ scenes (crash, kindergarten, the truth in the car, the phone call, the finale).

## Props checklist
- [ ] **One paperback book** labeled *The Short Stories of Nikolai Gogol*. It is in scenes 1, 4, 6 and 8 and ties the whole thing together.
- [ ] Envelope with a red stamp (the lost letter)
- [ ] "HELLO my name is" name tags, one reading **GOGOL** and one reading **NIKHIL**
- [ ] Flashlight (and a second phone flashlight)
- [ ] A printed "birth certificate" and a "name change petition" form
- [ ] Wrapping paper, a birthday candle, a backpack, a razor (unused, just for the shot), a phone
- [ ] Phone speaker to play train sounds (search "train interior ambience")

## Visual rules (so all the videos match)
- **1961 flashback = black & white.** Everything else is in color.
- **Gogol scenes are warm**, with lamps and golden light. **Nikhil scenes are cool**, with daylight, blue light and a colder filter.
- **End every video on a close-up of a name**: the page, the birth certificate, a name tag, the form, the inscription. We can cut these together as a finale montage.
- Add a title card at the start of each video: the year and the scene title, white typewriter text on black.

## Filming day schedule (about 3–4 hours)
Film by **location**, not in story order:

| Time | Location | Scenes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Setup | Charge phones, set out props, test lighting |
| 0:20–1:20 | **Bedroom** | 2 (hospital), 4 (gift), 8 (finale) |
| 1:20–1:50 | **Dark room + chairs** | 1 (train crash) |
| 1:50–2:30 | **Classroom/desk + party room** | 3 (kindergarten), 5 (Nikhil) |
| 2:30–3:00 | **Apartment + mirror** | 7 (phone call) |
| 3:00–3:40 | **Car (after dark)** | 6 (the truth), saved for last so it's dark outside |

## Filming tips
- **Record 3 takes** of every line, since it's much easier to pick the best one while editing.
- Film **5 extra seconds** before and after each shot so transitions have room.
- Keep the phone **steady** except in the crash scene. Lean it on something if there's no tripod.
- Record audio close to the actors, or film the dialogue close-ups tighter.
- Export as **MP4, 1080p or 720p**, and keep each file under ~100 MB.
